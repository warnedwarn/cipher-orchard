# Frontend Design Contract

## screenArchitecture
Puzzle-box-interface.

## aboveTheFold
A branching specimen fills the center. Public clues appear as pinned seed packets, branch sockets show accepted grafts, and a root-level graft tray accepts the next move.

## primaryInteraction
A unique wallet proposes one semantic graft. Consensus grows a branch or spends one of the specimen's limited blight marks.

## layoutMap
An offset herbarium title, a left seed drawer, a central branching specimen, a right season dial, and a low graft tray. Mobile uses a single vertical specimen scroll.

## visualMetaphor
A cryptobotanical specimen table where semantic answers become living grafts.

## motionSystem
Branch Growth, Pollen Drift, Graft Snap, Consensus Germination, and Blight Recoil.

## effectStack
Procedural pollen canvas and CSS branch growth paths.

## componentShapeLanguage
Pressed specimen labels, seed packets, branch sockets, uneven paper cuts, and heavy botanical rules.

## customVisualComponents
PollenField, BranchSpecimen, SeedDrawer, SeasonDial, GraftTray.

## bannedFromThisBuild
No generic hero, equal cards, horizontal stats, chip rows, submit-to-feed layout, three-column footer, dark navy, glassmorphism, contour map, or dispatch rail.

## proofOfDifference
Faultline Atlas is an angular municipal map with sector selection. Cipher Orchard is a radial cooperative puzzle centered on organic growth, branch sockets, and finite blight.
